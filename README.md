# Privacy Policy for 12 Tenses Sinhala

**Effective Date:** November 17, 2026

**Developer:** Studio Aiva (`com.studioaiva.tenses12lk`)

Thank you for using **12 Tenses Sinhala** ("the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines what information the App collects and how it is used.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use

**12 Tenses Sinhala** does not require user registration or account creation. We do not directly collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical location).

### 2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The App integrates **Google AdMob** to display banner and interstitial advertisements, which helps support free access to our educational content.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain device information, such as:
- Advertising Identifier (e.g., Google Advertising ID / AAID)
- IP address (used for approximate coarse location / ad serving)
- Device model, operating system, and hardware specifications
- App interaction and ad performance metrics

This data is handled according to Google's Privacy Policy:
- [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

### 3. Children’s Privacy

The App is designed for general audiences and language learners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information to third-party services through our App, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

### 4. Security

We value your trust. Since we do not collect or store user accounts or personal profiles on private servers, your exposure to data breaches through this App is virtually nonexistent.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
